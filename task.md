# WEBSITE REVISION — VEER FURNITURE MALL LOHGAON
## Owner Feedback: Reduce Clutter, Improve Layout & Refine User Experience

Act as a senior UI/UX designer, creative director and senior React frontend engineer.

I have received feedback from the owner after reviewing the current website.

The owner likes the premium direction but has identified three major problems:

1. The website looks too crowded on desktop and mobile.
2. The content cards are excessively tall and contain too much text.
3. The preloader appears too quickly and does not feel sufficiently polished.

Your task is to refine the existing website based on this feedback.

## 1. IMPORTANT PROJECT RULES

Current website:
https://veer-furniture-kappa.vercel.app/

Layout reference:
https://veer-furniture-chakan.street-up.in/

The Chakan website should be used as a reference for:
- Overall website section structure.
- Information architecture.
- Content organization.
- Furniture category presentation.
- Product browsing layout.
- Card proportions and content density.
- Showroom gallery presentation.
- Reviews and contact section organization.

IMPORTANT DESIGN DISTINCTION:

Do not copy the Chakan website's visual theme, colour palette, typography, logo treatment or exact styling.

Use its layout and content organization as inspiration while maintaining the independent Lohgaon brand identity.

The current Lohgaon website already has a charcoal/navy, gold and ivory visual direction.

Preserve and refine this visual identity.

Do not redesign everything unnecessarily.

## 2. FIRST INSPECT THE EXISTING PROJECT

Before changing code:

- Inspect the complete React + TypeScript project.
- Inspect existing components.
- Inspect current CSS and Tailwind classes.
- Inspect current images and logo.
- Identify excessively large components.
- Identify repeated styling.
- Check responsive behaviour.
- Identify which sections create visual clutter.

Do not replace the current application architecture.

Do not remove working functionality.

Make targeted improvements.

## 3. GLOBAL DESIGN REFINEMENT

The website currently contains too many visual elements competing for attention.

Create more breathing room.

Improve:
- Section spacing.
- Content hierarchy.
- Typography hierarchy.
- Image proportions.
- Alignment.
- Component dimensions.
- Text density.
- Visual balance.

Use negative space intentionally.

Not every section needs multiple decorative elements.

Not every section needs cards.

Not every component needs a border or background.

Make the website feel calm, premium, elegant and professionally designed.

Maintain consistent section widths and alignment.

Reduce unnecessary decorative elements.

## 4. NAVBAR REFINEMENT

The current navigation contains too many items competing for horizontal space.

Refine it.

Desktop:
- Keep the original Veer logo.
- Keep the brand name and Lohgaon identification.
- Home.
- About.
- Collections.
- Gallery.
- Reviews.
- Location.
- One primary showroom CTA.
- Phone contact displayed compactly.

Requirements:
- Reduce unnecessary gaps and oversized navigation labels.
- Use consistent typography.
- Maintain alignment.
- Ensure the header does not feel compressed.
- Keep the navbar sticky if already implemented.

Mobile:
- Display the logo and a menu button.
- Move navigation links into an elegant slide-in drawer.
- Put contact and showroom actions inside the drawer.
- Avoid a crowded horizontal navbar.
- Ensure there is no horizontal overflow.

## 5. HERO SECTION — REDUCE VISUAL CLUTTER

The current hero has:
- Background furniture photograph.
- Large headline.
- Supporting description.
- Two buttons.
- Large product showcase card.
- Product specifications.
- Additional inquiry details.
- Three statistics.
- Floating WhatsApp notification.

This is too much for one screen.

Redesign the hero with fewer competing elements.

Recommended composition:

LEFT:
- Small location eyebrow.
- Large elegant heading.
- Short supporting description.
- Primary CTA: Explore Collections.
- Secondary CTA: Visit Showroom.

RIGHT:
- One large premium furniture photograph.
- Optional small, unobtrusive image label.

Below the hero:
- A compact trust-information strip, only if its information is verified.

Remove unnecessary product specifications from the hero.

Do not display a large product information card on top of another large background photograph.

Keep the hero visually impressive without filling every available space.

The first screen should communicate the brand, product offering and showroom location immediately.

Make a separate intentional mobile hero composition instead of shrinking the desktop composition.

## 6. MAKE ALL CONTENT CARDS SHORTER

This is one of the owner's primary complaints.

Reduce the height of all unnecessarily tall cards.

Apply this particularly to:
- Furniture categories.
- Product collections.
- Service cards.
- Gallery captions.
- Testimonial cards.

Recommended category card design:

- Image at the top.
- Short category title.
- One or two lines of description.
- Small CTA or arrow.

Do not include long paragraphs inside cards.

Use line clamping for descriptions.

Suggested proportions:

DESKTOP:
- Image height: approximately 170–210px.
- Text area: approximately 85–110px.
- Total card height: approximately 270–330px.

TABLET:
- Image height: approximately 145–175px.
- Compact content area.

MOBILE:
- Prefer two-column compact cards where image clarity permits.
- Image height: approximately 115–145px.
- Short title and one-line description.
- Avoid tall vertical cards.

These are target dimensions, not rigid rules. Adjust based on the actual images and available width.

Use consistent image aspect ratios.

Do not distort images.

Avoid long descriptions, multiple metadata rows and several buttons inside a single card.

## 7. CONTENT REDUCTION

Review all website copy.

The current presentation contains too much information in certain areas.

Make content more concise without removing useful business information.

Rules:

- Headings should be short and meaningful.
- Paragraphs should be easy to scan.
- Avoid repeating the same message across sections.
- Avoid unnecessary promotional statements.
- Avoid long descriptions inside product cards.
- Use short captions where appropriate.
- Keep CTAs descriptive and direct.

Do not invent product specifications or business claims.

Verify any ratings, number of customers, opening hours, product features and warranty statements before retaining them.

## 8. FOLLOW THE REFERENCE WEBSITE'S CONTENT FLOW

Use the Chakan website as a structural reference and organize the Lohgaon website with the following flow:

1. Home / Hero
2. Our Story / About
3. What We Offer
4. Our Showroom / Gallery
5. Browse Our Collection
6. Customer Reviews
7. Visit Our Store
8. Contact / Footer

Maintain original Lohgaon business information, photographs and reviews.

Never use Chakan branch business details or testimonials.

The sections should not all look alike.

Create visual variety without creating unnecessary complexity.

## 9. GALLERY REFINEMENT

Keep the gallery visually rich but well organized.

Use:
- Balanced image grids.
- Consistent aspect ratios.
- Carefully selected photographs.
- Short image captions.
- Subtle hover effects.
- Lightbox functionality.

Avoid very tall gallery cards and excessive text below images.

The gallery should primarily showcase furniture, not descriptions.

## 10. REVIEWS REFINEMENT

Use only authentic Lohgaon customer reviews.

Display reviews in compact, readable cards.

Each review should contain:
- Customer name, where publicly available.
- Actual star rating.
- A concise original review excerpt.
- Attribution or link to the source.

Do not rewrite original reviews and present them as exact quotations.

Do not fabricate reviews.

Use a simple, elegant slider or a compact grid.

Avoid oversized testimonial cards.

## 11. FLOATING WHATSAPP CTA

The current WhatsApp notification pop-up adds unnecessary visual clutter.

Remove automatic notification pop-ups that obscure the page.

Instead:

- Keep a small floating WhatsApp button.
- Use a minimal icon and optional short label.
- Make it responsive.
- Avoid covering content or other buttons.
- Use the verified Lohgaon contact number.
- Keep the CTA functional.

## 12. PRELOADER — MORE POLISHED AND SLIGHTLY SLOWER

The current preloader disappears too quickly.

Create a more professional and deliberate preloader experience.

Use the existing Veer Furniture Mall transparent PNG logo.

IMPORTANT:
- Do not redraw the logo.
- Do not change its typography.
- Do not change its colours.
- Do not add a brown background.
- Do not distort its proportions.
- Preserve the original logo asset.

Creative direction:

1. Start with a clean charcoal/navy background.
2. Reveal the original logo smoothly.
3. Use a subtle opacity and scale animation.
4. Introduce a refined gold progress indicator.
5. Use elegant easing.
6. Transition smoothly into the homepage.

Timing:
- Aim for approximately 1.8–2.3 seconds on a normal initial load.
- Coordinate the animation with critical hero asset loading.
- Do not artificially delay the website for several seconds.
- Provide a sensible upper time limit.
- Do not wait for every below-the-fold image.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

The preloader should feel like a premium brand introduction, not a loading inconvenience.

Do not use a default spinning circle.
